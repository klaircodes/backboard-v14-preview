Unicorn Founder Rob Imbeault Returns to Build Backboard.io; The Memory Layer for AI

AI is brilliant, but it forgets. SaaS Unicorn founder Rob Imbeault thinks that’s the biggest problem in the stack. Backed by Mistral, N49P, Garage Capital and DevCap, his new company Backboard is building the memory layer AI has been missing.

Secures Strategic Backing from Mistral Venture Partners, n49p, Garage Capital and DevCap

TORONTO, ON — January 6th, 2026 — Backboard.io, an AI infrastructure company building a memory-native layer for Large Language Models (LLMs), today announced a substantial pre-seed funding round co-led by Mistral Venture Partners (Cohere, Klipfolio) and N49P (Spellbook, EvenUP). The round includes key participation from Garage Capital (Groq, Substack) and Dev Cap (Unified, Glowtify).

Founded by Rob Imbeault, co-founder of a SaaS unicorn Assent, Backboard.io marks his return to the operator’s seat to address one of the most pressing bottlenecks in generative AI: the lack of stateful, persistent, and portable memory.

Imbeault previously helped scale Assent from a basement startup into a global supply chain compliance platform valued at more than $1.4 billion. He is now applying the same enterprise-grade rigor around scalability, security, and data reliability to the fragmented AI infrastructure stack.

The Mission: Memory-Native AI Infrastructure

Despite rapid advances in model performance, today’s AI systems suffer from a fundamental limitation: amnesia. Once a session ends, context is lost, forcing developers to rely on complex and brittle workarounds to preserve state across interactions.

Backboard.io addresses this challenge by providing a unified, portable memory layer for AI, enabling persistent context and stateful orchestration across more than 2,000 models. Backboard’s memory system has already demonstrated industry-leading performance, achieving 90.1% accuracy on the LoCoMo (Long Conversational Memory) benchmark.

About Backboard.io

Backboard.io is a developer-first AI infrastructure platform designed to serve as the persistent state layer for Large Language Models (LLMs). Currently ranked #1 on the LoCoMo benchmark, the platform enables true memory-native AI systems, moving beyond simple conversational history to retain high-fidelity, long-term context required for complex SaaS tools and autonomous agents. Backboard.io supports multi-model orchestration across more than 2,000 LLMs, providing a portable memory layer that works across providers rather than locking developers into a single vendor. By combining persistent AI memory, context management, and stateful orchestration, Backboard allows teams to deploy a secure, production-ready AI stack in minutes. The company is focused on solving the industry's "stateless" problem: giving AI the ability to remember, retrieve, and build on past interactions to support sophisticated, multi-step workflows.

About Backboard.io

Backboard.io is an AI infrastructure company that puts the power of advanced AI in the hands of the companies and developers who use it. We build infrastructure that runs under the customer's own control, with security and privacy by design. Our platform helps enterprises and developers deploy advanced AI affordably and securely, without giving up ownership of their data.
